Welcome to Humline

This is an early test version. Sing, hum, tap, or play a riff, and Humline turns it into notes you can build a song around.

  1. Pick what you're recording. Notes is for singing, humming, or guitar and bass played one note at a time. Rhythm is for tapping or beatboxing. Just record keeps the sound as it is, which is the one to use for strummed chords.
  2. Press Record. You'll hear four clicks to count you in, then it records for the length you picked.
  3. Press Play, then try the Arrange buttons on the right to add bass, drums and chords. Record more takes on top whenever you like.

Chrome will ask to use your microphone the first time. Choose Allow. Headphones help, because they keep the click and the other parts out of your recording.

Your song saves itself in this browser. When you've had a play, press Send feedback and say what worked and what didn't.
